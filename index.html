FIX IT is being built around nearby discovery. Location can help people find relevant skills, goods and services without having to search an entire country.

Nearby discovery

Search results can be narrowed by location so nearby options are easier to find.

Privacy first

Precise location should be used for useful discovery without making a person's exact location publicly visible.

Real-world usefulness

From a repair to an errand, a local service or a product, FIX IT is designed around things people actually need.
